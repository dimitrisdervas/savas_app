# Project Goals

## Primary Objectives

### 1. Comprehensive Cardiovascular Risk Assessment
- Implement latest European Society of Cardiology guidelines (ESC 2021/2023)
- Support multiple risk calculation algorithms (SCORE2, SCORE2-OP, SCORE2-Diabetes, ASCVD)
- Provide age-appropriate & condition-specific assessments
- Enable accurate 10-year CV risk prediction

### 2. Integrated Kidney Disease Risk Stratification
- Calculate kidney function using latest CKD-EPI 2021 equation (no race adjustment)
- Assess albuminuria severity via ACR
- Implement KDIGO risk matrix for comprehensive CKD staging
- Visualize GFR × ACR interactions

### 3. Holistic Cardio-Nephro-Metabolic Approach
- Combine CV risk, kidney function, & metabolic syndrome assessment
- Recognize cardiorenal syndrome interactions
- Support treatment decisions based on integrated risk profile
- Align w/ ESC 2023 LDL-C treatment targets

### 4. Support Both Diabetic & Non-Diabetic Patients
- Diabetes-specific CV risk calculations (SCORE2-Diabetes)
- Metabolic syndrome screening (IDF criteria)
- HbA1c & glucose monitoring integration
- Age of diabetes diagnosis tracking

### 5. Efficient Family Practice Workflow
- Single-page interface for rapid data entry
- Keyboard navigation for speed
- Real-time calculations (no manual compute step)
- Print-ready reports for patient records
- Multiple patient report stacking
- Offline capability (no internet required)

### 6. Evidence-Based Clinical Reports
- Clear risk stratification w/ color coding
- Personalized LDL-C targets based on calculated risk
- Comprehensive output including all relevant parameters
- Timestamp documentation
- Print-optimized formatting

### 7. Greek Medical Terminology Support
- Greek language interface for local practitioners
- Culturally appropriate (Greek population risk calibration)
- Familiar terminology for Dr. Savas Yialavouzis & colleagues

### 8. Latest Guideline Adherence
- SCORE2/SCORE2-OP/SCORE2-Diabetes (ESC 2021)
- CKD-EPI 2021 (no race adjustment)
- KDIGO CKD classification
- ESC 2023 dyslipidemia guidelines
- IDF metabolic syndrome criteria (European cutoffs)

### 9. Clinical Utility
- Enable early detection of high-risk patients
- Guide treatment intensification decisions
- Support cardiovascular & renal protection strategies
- Facilitate patient education & shared decision-making

### 10. Technical Excellence
- Self-contained (zero external dependencies)
- Fast loading & execution
- Browser-agnostic (modern standards)
- Maintainable single-file architecture
- Print-first design philosophy
